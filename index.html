Point 03 · Theological Clarity Colossians 1:15

The True Meaning of 'Firstborn' (Prototokos)

In ancient biblical culture, 'firstborn' (Greek: Prototokos) was never about chronological birth order or being the first created thing. Rather, it was a title of supreme ranking, sovereignty, inheritance, and universal authority. Christ is 'firstborn' because He stands over and prior to all creation as its Sovereign Heir and Originator.

⚖

Supreme Heir, Not First Creation

You cannot be created through someone who is created. Paul explicitly clarifies: 'For by Him ALL things were created.' He is the Creator, not part of the creaturely inventory.

Sovereignty

Supreme Position

Holding the preeminent place of honour and headship above every created dominion.

Eternity

Uncreated Source

Existing prior to the inception of time, nature, and the cosmic order.

He is the original spring from which all stars, galaxies, organic life, and spiritual realms flow into existence.

02 ⬡
THROUGH HIM

03 ☀

04 ∞
