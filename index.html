John 4:24

God Is Spirit

God, by His very nature, is spirit — invisible, untouchable, transcendent, beyond every physical boundary. No human eye has ever seen Him. No hand has reached Him. He dwells in light unapproachable; a purity so absolute that no created being can stand in His presence.

"No one has ever seen God."

— John 1:18

"God is light; in him there is no darkness at all."

— 1 John 1:5
Hebrews 1:3

The Radiance of His Glory

"The Son is the radiance of God's glory and the exact representation of his being, sustaining all things by his powerful word."

— Hebrews 1:3

Consider light and the sun. The sun itself is unapproachable — but its radiance, its light, is the very expression of what the sun is. The light carries the full reality of the sun to the world. Without the light, the sun is unknowable.

So the Son is to the Father. He is not a diminished version of God — He is the living radiance, the full and exact expression of God's own being, sent into the world so that God could finally be seen.

The Radiance of God

John 1:1–3

Before Creation

Before the first atom. Before the first photon of light. Before time had a beginning — the Word already existed. Not created at the beginning; already there, already eternal, already God, face to face with the Father.

"Through him all things were made; without him nothing was made that has been made." Every star, every galaxy, every breath of life — created through the Word.

"In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

— John 1:1
