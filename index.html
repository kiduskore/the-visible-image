Hebrews 1:3 · The Visible Expression

The Visible Expression of the Invisible

Because God is spirit and invisible, He chose to reveal Himself perfectly through the Son. The Son is the exact, visible representation of God's being. If anyone wants to see what the invisible God is like, they look at the Son.

"The Son is the radiance of God's glory and the exact representation of his being." — Hebrews 1:3

John 1:1–3 · Before All Things

I The Son existed in eternity past — before the physical world began
II He was the manifestation of God's glory before the universe even began
III Often called the Word, or Logos — He is the eternal self-expression of God

Colossians 1:15–16 · The Foundation

The Foundation of Everything

Because the Son is the visible image of God who existed before all things, He is the "firstborn" — not in the sense of being created, but in the sense that He is the source, the blueprint, and the ultimate purpose for why creation exists at all.

The SourceAll things were created by Him — He is the origin of everything
The BlueprintAll things were created through Him — He is the pattern for existence
The PurposeAll things were created for Him — He is the reason everything exists

The Conclusion · Colossians 1:15–17

These Titles Do Not Demote Jesus from Being God

"Firstborn," "Image," "Radiance" — these titles describe how the invisible God chose to manifest Himself and bring the entire universe into existence. They reveal not a lesser being, but the full, visible expression of the Almighty.
